AI-assisted travel platform

Eastern Wander

Why I built it

I wanted prospective travellers to understand what a private China itinerary would look like, then send an inquiry without moving between unrelated tools. The people handling trips also needed to see what came in and act on it.

How I designed it

I mapped the journey from discovering an itinerary to submitting an inquiry. I used AI-assisted development to build more than 30 pages and a back-office workflow, separating the browsing experience, inquiry submission and follow-up records.

What I verified

A test submission reached the database and appeared in the admin view. I treat that as separate evidence from whether an email was delivered.

What I would check next

I would test each step independently: the visitor sees a useful confirmation, the inquiry appears in the admin view, and the intended message actually arrives.
