Job Tracker

Why I built it

Job discovery, résumé tailoring and application history were scattered across different tools. I wanted to see exactly where a role was in the process without treating every listing as a match or every click as an application.

How I designed it

I separated discovery, deduplication, résumé fit, eligibility and application evidence. The same job should not produce another record just because it appears again. An updated or reposted vacancy is not automatically a new one. I used Codex to implement and test the local workbench and its status dashboard.

What I verified

The local dashboard and status interface worked. I tested deduplication and record handling, and verified that the review queue could save a résumé with synthetic data. Final submissions remain manual.

What is still being tested

Scheduled discovery and live automatic résumé generation are not presented as fully validated.
